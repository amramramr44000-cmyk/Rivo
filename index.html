ABOUT RIVO

One local app, five real tools

Rivo is a complete local-first identity platform: create an account, then build your public profile inside a full editor — choose a card template, an avatar frame, a theme color and a card style, then drag sections into the order you want.

From there, Explore helps people find you by username or name, Friends handles requests and your circle, and Settings covers your account. Profiles, social data, settings and media are stored remotely so the same account works across devices.

EditorTemplates, frames, themes, card styles and reorderable sections, with a live preview beside every change.
ExploreSearch people by username or display name and open their public card directly.
FriendsSend, accept and browse friend requests, and keep track of your circle.
Projects & musicFeature project cards with thumbnails and links, plus a music player styled to match your card.
SettingsManage your account details from one place.
Cloud databaseAccounts, friends and profiles are saved on your device — fast, private, and available offline.
