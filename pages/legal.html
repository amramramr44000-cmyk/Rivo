Terms of Use

Using Rivo

Use Rivo lawfully and respectfully. Do not impersonate others, abuse accounts, bypass security controls, interfere with the service, or use Rivo to distribute unlawful, fraudulent, hateful or harmful content.

Your account

You are responsible for activity performed through your account and for keeping your credentials secure. Rivo may restrict or remove content or accounts that violate these rules, applicable law, or the safety of the service.

Privacy Policy

Information we handle

Rivo may process account identifiers, profile information, social interactions, uploaded media, settings, and technical information required to operate, secure and improve the service. Authentication and cloud data are handled through the configured Rivo/Supabase infrastructure.

Public and private information

Rivo should not expose private account fields such as passwords or service secrets through a public profile. Some information is public because users intentionally publish it on their profile or in public communities.

Community Rules

  • Respect other people and do not harass or threaten them.
  • Do not spam, scam, or deliberately manipulate social or game systems.
  • Do not share someone else's private information without permission.
  • Use moderation and reporting tools responsibly.

Security & Account Safety

Keep credentials private

Never share your password. Sensitive server credentials must remain outside the browser. Security controls may include authentication, rate limits, human checks, database permissions and server-side authorization.

Contact & Changes

Rivo may update these documents when features, security practices or legal requirements change. The published version on this page is the version intended to be presented to users.
